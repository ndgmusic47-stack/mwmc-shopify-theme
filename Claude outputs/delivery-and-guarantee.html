The Hassle-Free Handover Guarantee

If a statue arrives damaged, a listed part is missing, or the piece is not as described, we arrange and pay for its collection and refund you in full. If we are storing your original packaging, we will bring it. This sits alongside your statutory rights, which are unaffected.

Getting it home

Free collection in London

Collection from our London base costs nothing and is usually ready within 24 hours of your order. We confirm a time with you by email.

Personal delivery

We deliver statues ourselves rather than handing them to a parcel carrier. One of us drives the piece to you, carries it in, and waits while you look it over.

Your price is shown on the statue's own page before you add it to your basket, and it is the price Shopify charges at checkout. Enter your postcode and you will see the figure straight away.

How the price is worked out

Personal delivery is priced by the round-trip road distance between our dispatch point and your address: the drive out and the drive back, added together. It does not change with the value of the piece, so a £150 statue and a £1,400 statue cost the same to bring to the same door.

Addresses we price by hand

Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man, the Channel Islands and the Scottish islands need a ferry, and a few remote mainland addresses sit beyond the table. If your postcode is one of them the statue's page will say so and offer you a quote instead of a price. Ask us and we will come back to you with a figure.

Optional extras

We set it up for you — £49

We unbox the piece, assemble it, and put it exactly where you want it before we leave. Tick it on the statue's page and it is added to your order and paid with it.

We keep your box — first month free, then £19.99 a month

Original boxes are bulky and worth keeping, because a boxed statue is worth more than an unboxed one. We store yours and bring it back whenever you want it. Your first month is free from the day we collect the box. Tell us at handover and we will confirm the start date, the terms and how the box comes back to you, in writing, before anything is charged. You can stop at any time.

Selling it back to us

When you are ready to sell, we will buy your statue back at the price we quote after checking its condition, what is included and current resale value. Getting the quote is free. You decide whether to accept. Any collection charge is shown separately on the quote.
